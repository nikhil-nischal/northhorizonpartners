Hippocratic AI

A safety-focused generative AI company backed by Andreessen Horowitz and General Catalyst opened two partnership conversations that compress years of roadmap into a quarter. Not lead flow. Strategic relationships in a market that closes on trust.

2 partnerships worth $35,000 in 60 days
Munjal Shah
Co-founder & CEO, Hippocratic AI

Client

Hippocratic AI

Category

Healthcare AI

Result

2 · partnerships worth $35,000 in 60 days

About the client

Hippocratic AI builds safety-focused generative AI that helps hospitals and providers make better decisions and improve patient care. The technology was strong; the market is crowded and slow to trust.

Inbound and traditional marketing were not producing conversations fast enough, and the hospital decision-makers who mattered were the hardest people to reach.

The objective

Open partnership-grade conversations with hospital administrators, technology leaders, and healthcare innovators: the people whose signature moves a roadmap.

What we did

Pinpoint the ideal partners

Not "healthcare companies." The specific administrators, technology leaders, and innovators whose problems matched what the product actually does.

Make outreach feel one-to-one

Personalized, genuine messages built around each prospect's situation, not a template with a first-name token.

Never drop a live conversation

Smart automation tracked every thread and handled timely follow-ups, with a nurturing process that kept interested prospects moving.

The outcome

  • 2 new partnership deals worth $35,000 within 60 days
  • A quicker sales process and a more confident team approach to outreach
  • A clear, repeatable growth motion replacing slow, uncertain inbound

When the partner is right, the roadmap moves a year forward.
