HelpCare AI

A healthcare AI company secured four key clients through strategic introductions to the exact decision-makers who needed the product, in a market that is notoriously hard to penetrate.

4 key healthcare clients secured

HelpCare AI

About the client

HelpCare AI builds AI-driven solutions for patient care and operational efficiency. They needed key clients to validate the product in a competitive, hard-to-penetrate healthcare market.

Despite the strength of the technology, they struggled to reach the right decision-makers and demonstrate its full potential.

The objective

Open doors to the healthcare organizations with a specific, present need for what the product does, and arrive with credibility already established.

What we did

Introduce through the network

We used our established healthcare network to introduce HelpCare AI to targeted clients chosen for their specific need for AI in patient care optimization, real-time monitoring, and predictive analytics.

Align to each prospect's pain

Highly personalized one-to-one outreach engaged key decision-makers by mapping the product to each prospect's actual situation, backed by proof.

Qualify and nurture

A qualification and nurturing process kept the pipeline moving and the conversations warm.

The outcome

  • 4 key healthcare clients secured through strategic introductions
  • Lasting client relationships positioning the company as a trusted partner
  • Increased market presence, opening doors to referrals and further opportunities

When trust is borrowed well, healthcare doors open.
