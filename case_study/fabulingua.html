FabuLingua

A children's language-learning platform landed five new clients in 48 days on an $8,288 investment, without burning more ad budget.

5 new clients in 48 days

Client

FabuLingua

Category

EdTech

Result

5 · new clients in 48 days

About the client

FabuLingua teaches languages to kids through storytelling and interactive learning.

Their inbound channels were slow and unpredictable at producing qualified opportunities, and they were spending heavily on ads while struggling to consistently reach the educators and decision-makers who actually buy.

The objective

Reach the schools, learning centers, and parents who were already looking for engaging educational tools, directly and personally.

What we did

Define the real buyer

Schools, language learning centers, and parents actively seeking engaging educational tools: a precise profile instead of an ad audience.

Write like a person

Personalized, multi-step outreach that felt one-to-one, not mass-sent.

Automate the follow-through

Smart automation handled capture, follow-ups, and pipeline tracking, with a nurturing process guiding prospects toward conversion.

The outcome

  • 5 new clients acquired in 48 days of high-intensity campaigns
  • Strong return on an $8,288 initial investment
  • Faster pipeline movement and a shorter sales cycle
  • A repeatable outbound system for ongoing growth

When the buyer is right, ad spend stops mattering.
