Elate Staffing Solutions

A UK recruitment agency moved from fully manual prospecting to an automated, trigger-driven system: 22 placements in 55 days and a 50% lift in conversions.

22 placements in 55 days

Client

Elate Staffing Solutions

Category

Industrial Recruitment (UK)

Result

22 · placements in 55 days

About the client

Elate Staffing serves the Industrial, Technical & Engineering, and Commercial sectors in the UK. Prospecting and outreach ran almost entirely by hand.

Qualification was inconsistent, response times were slow, and there was no scalable way to reach the HR and hiring managers who make the decisions.

The objective

Build a system that finds the right hiring decision-makers, reaches them at the right moment, and never lets a warm response go cold.

What we did

Identify the decision-makers

An automated system to identify HR managers, hiring managers, and decision-makers across their target sectors, verified across multiple data sources.

Trigger on live vacancies

Outreach fired when companies posted live vacancies, during optimal weekday hours, backed by full email authentication for deliverability.

Manage the pipeline like an operation

Response categorization and automated alerts cut follow-up times from days to hours.

The outcome

  • 22 placements in 55 days, surpassing initial targets
  • 50% increase in conversions over the previous manual process
  • Response times cut from days to hours
  • Scaled outreach without adding headcount

When targeting goes live, conversions double.
