ForPlayers

The campaign

About the client

ForPlayers is a prominent esports organization spanning competitive gaming, sponsorships, and content creation.

They could not maintain a steady stream of high-quality prospects: conversion rates were low, and the sales team was losing time on low-priority conversations while high-value sponsorship opportunities slipped by.

The objective

Keep a steady flow of sponsor-grade conversations moving: brands, gaming companies, and collaborators who fit the audience.

What we did

Define the high-value prospect

Brands wanting a digital-first esports audience, gaming companies seeking promotional partners, and influencers open to collaboration.

Personalize and follow through

Personalized, automated outreach with follow-up sequences and streamlined meeting scheduling with clear calls to action.

Test relentlessly

Subject lines and copy A/B tested continuously to keep meeting conversion improving.

The outcome

  • €70,000 in new revenue in three months from booked meetings and conversions
  • A more efficient, scalable system for securing qualified meetings
  • Stronger sponsor and partner relationships, opening long-term collaboration

When the sponsor fits, the deal follows.
