Lasting Blueprint Productions

An Orlando video production company stopped selling "video" and started reaching organizations with real activity that needed documenting. Four qualified introductions followed in 78 days.

About the client

Lasting Blueprint Productions serves Orlando and Central Florida: events, corporate, training, testimonials, promos.

The problem: "anyone who needs video" is not a market. Nobody wakes up thinking they need video production, and a category that broad creates no urgency at all.

The objective

Reposition the target from "video buyers" to organizations with visible activity already happening that needed professional documentation.

What we did

Find the activity, not the buyer

Conference and event teams in one of the country's strongest convention markets, nonprofits needing donor and program proof, trust-driven healthcare and professional-services firms, and training-heavy construction and field-service companies.

Route at visible triggers

Outreach timed to upcoming events, annual conferences, and training programs: moments when documentation is already on someone's list.

Use every unfair advantage

The company's MBE and DBE certifications opened a public-sector-adjacent lane with agencies, universities, and larger contractors.

The outcome

  • 4 qualified introductions routed directly in 78 days
  • A generic service category narrowed into concrete buying situations with visible triggers
  • A repeatable positioning frame: documenting business activity that is already happening

When the activity is real, the camera gets hired.
